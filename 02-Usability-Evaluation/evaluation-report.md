# Usability Evaluation Report – Hawkeye Travel App

## 1. Introduction

Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) focuses on designing digital systems that are easy, effective, and comfortable for people to use.

This report presents a preliminary usability evaluation of the Hawkeye Travel App UI Kit. The evaluation focuses on five selected screens: Login Page, Schedule Page, Personal Center, Travel Arrangement Page, and Friend Communication Page.

The objective is to examine the interface from a user's perspective, identify potential usability issues, and suggest improvements.

## 2. Selected Application Information

- **Design Name:** Hawkeye Travel App Template
- **Designer:** Alan Love
- **Category:** Travel Planning Mobile Application
- **Design Source:** Free Figma Templates
- **Design Tool:** Figma
- **Design Link:** https://www.freefigmatemplates.com/gallery/travel-app-template

## 3. Purpose of the Application

The application is designed to help users manage their travel activities through a mobile interface.

Its main purposes include:

- Accessing personal accounts.
- Managing travel schedules.
- Organizing travel arrangements.
- Managing personal information.
- Communicating with friends.

## 4. Target Users

The application is intended for:

- Individual travelers.
- Friends planning trips together.
- People who organize travel schedules.
- Users who want to manage personal travel information.
- People who coordinate travel activities with others.

## 5. Evaluation Methodology

A preliminary inspection of five selected UI screens was used to examine the design.

The evaluation considers the following usability principles:

### 5.1 Learnability
How easily can a new user understand and use the interface?

### 5.2 Efficiency
Can users complete common activities without unnecessary steps?

### 5.3 Consistency
Are similar buttons, icons, and interface elements presented consistently?

### 5.4 Visibility
Can users easily identify important information and available actions?

### 5.5 Accessibility
Is the interface readable and understandable for users with different needs?

## 6. Screen-by-Screen Usability Evaluation

### 6.1 Login Page

**Purpose:** Allows users to access their accounts.

**Potential Usability Issues:**
- Small text may make some information difficult to read.
- Unclear error messages could confuse users.
- Important account recovery options may not be sufficiently noticeable.

**Suggested Improvements:**
- Use readable font sizes.
- Provide clear validation messages.
- Make account recovery options easy to locate.
- Use clearly labeled input fields and buttons.

### 6.2 Schedule Page

**Purpose:** Allows users to view and organize travel schedules.

**Potential Usability Issues:**
- Too much information on one screen may make the schedule difficult to understand.
- Important dates and activities may not stand out sufficiently.
- Users may need clearer indicators for upcoming activities.

**Suggested Improvements:**
- Organize activities by date and time.
- Use clear headings and visual separation.
- Highlight upcoming activities.
- Provide an easy way to edit or update schedules.

### 6.3 Personal Center

**Purpose:** Provides access to personal information and account settings.

**Potential Usability Issues:**
- Important account options may be difficult to locate.
- Similar menu items may create confusion.
- Users may need clearer labels for settings.

**Suggested Improvements:**
- Group related settings together.
- Use understandable labels and recognizable icons.
- Maintain consistent spacing between menu items.
- Make important account options easy to find.

### 6.4 Travel Arrangement Page

**Purpose:** Helps users organize their travel plans.

**Potential Usability Issues:**
- Travel details may become difficult to manage if not properly organized.
- Important trip information may not be sufficiently highlighted.
- Users may need clear options for modifying travel arrangements.

**Suggested Improvements:**
- Organize travel details into separate sections.
- Highlight important trip information.
- Provide clear edit and update options.
- Present dates, locations, and activities in an organized format.

### 6.5 Friend Communication Page

**Purpose:** Supports communication and coordination between friends.

**Potential Usability Issues:**
- Important messages may be difficult to distinguish.
- Conversation information may become confusing without clear visual separation.
- Users may need better visibility of communication options.

**Suggested Improvements:**
- Organize conversations clearly.
- Distinguish sent and received messages.
- Provide recognizable communication controls.
- Maintain readable text and sufficient spacing.

## 7. Overall Usability Findings

The preliminary evaluation identifies five important areas for improvement:

| Area | Improvement |
|---|---|
| Login | Clearer instructions and error feedback |
| Schedule | Better organization of dates and activities |
| Personal Center | More understandable settings navigation |
| Travel Arrangement | Clearer presentation of travel information |
| Friend Communication | Improved conversation organization |

These are potential usability concerns that should be checked against the actual screens before being treated as confirmed defects.

## 8. Recommended UX Improvements

The following recommendations could improve the overall user experience:

1. Maintain consistent typography and interface styling.
2. Use readable text sizes and appropriate color contrast.
3. Provide clear navigation labels.
4. Organize information into meaningful sections.
5. Provide feedback when users perform important actions.
6. Make important functions easy to locate.
7. Design interfaces that are understandable for first-time users.

## 9. Limitations

This report is based on a preliminary inspection of five static design screens from the Hawkeye Travel App UI Kit.

No real users were interviewed or tested, and no functional application was tested.

Therefore, the identified issues represent potential usability concerns rather than verified problems experienced by actual users.

## 10. Conclusion

The usability evaluation of the Hawkeye Travel App demonstrates the importance of user-centered design in mobile applications.

The five selected screens represent important application activities, including account access, scheduling, personal information management, travel planning, and communication.

Applying HCI principles can help improve the clarity, accessibility, consistency, and efficiency of these interfaces.

This activity helped develop an understanding of usability evaluation and the importance of designing applications according to users' needs.
